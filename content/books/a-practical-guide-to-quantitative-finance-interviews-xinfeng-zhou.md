**The "Green Book." The most-cited interview-prep reference in the industry.**

## Why Read This

If you are interviewing for any quant trader or quant researcher role, this is the standard problem set. Around 200 real interview problems organized by topic: brainteasers, calculus, linear algebra, probability, stochastic processes, finance, and programming. Every chapter starts with the minimum theory you need and then drills problems with worked solutions.

Treat it like LeetCode for quant interviews: do problems daily, simulate timing, and re-derive solutions from scratch rather than reading them passively.

## Key Takeaways

- Many interview problems collapse the moment you condition on the right variable — [[conditional-probability|Conditional Probability]] is the single most-tested skill.
- A small set of techniques recur constantly: indicator-variable expectations, symmetry arguments, recursive [[expected-value|Expected Value]], generating functions, [[bayes-theorem|Bayes' Theorem]] updates.
- Brainteasers are not "tricks" — they are testing whether you can structure an unfamiliar problem under time pressure while narrating your reasoning.
- Stochastic-calculus questions in the book preview what a real Black-Scholes / [[itos-lemma|Itô's Lemma]] derivation looks like under timed conditions.
