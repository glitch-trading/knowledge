**The original quant interview brainteaser collection. Updated annually for decades.**

## Why Read This

The oldest curated set of real quant interview questions, collected from interviews at investment banks, hedge funds, and trading firms. Lighter on theory than the [[a-practical-guide-to-quantitative-finance-interviews-xinfeng-zhou|Green Book]] but covers a broader surface area, including non-technical questions ("Why this firm?", "Walk me through your CV") and the older end of derivatives questions.

Best used alongside Zhou — Crack for breadth and brainteaser flavor, Zhou for depth and solution rigor.

## Key Takeaways

- Many classic brainteasers (rope-burning, weighing problems, hat puzzles, river crossings) appear repeatedly across firms — pattern recognition is half the battle.
- Crack documents non-technical questions explicitly, which the technical books skip. Be ready for "What's your edge?" as much as "Price this option."
- Derivatives questions in the book are an early sanity check on whether you actually understand [[options-futures-and-other-derivatives-john-hull|Hull]] beyond formula memorization.
